# Forge USP & Messaging

## USP 1 — Built around your workflow
**MOST SOFTWARE MAKES YOUR BUSINESS FIT THE SYSTEM.**
**FORGE BUILDS THE SYSTEM AROUND YOUR BUSINESS.**

## USP 2 — Context carries forward
**THE JOB MOVES.**
**THE CONTEXT COMES WITH IT.**

Capture information once and reuse it as the work progresses.

## USP 3 — Attention, not noise
**DON'T SHOW ME EVERYTHING.**
**SHOW ME WHAT NEEDS ME.**

Forge should surface exceptions and owner-required actions instead of forcing the owner to monitor everything.

## USP 4 — AI that can help act
**MOST AI GIVES YOU AN ANSWER.**
**FORGE CAN HELP DO THE NEXT THING.**

Examples:
- Which quotes over £5,000 need chasing?
- Draft the follow-ups.
- Which completed jobs have not been invoiced?
- Prepare the invoices. Do not send them.

## USP 5 — Keep what works
**KEEP WHAT WORKS.**
**CONNECT WHAT DOESN'T.**

Existing tools such as Xero, WhatsApp, email and calendars can remain where appropriate.

## Product behaviour language
Forge should increasingly be described using:
- CAPTURE
- UNDERSTAND
- CARRY
- WATCH
- PREPARE
- ACT
