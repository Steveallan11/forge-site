## Non-negotiables
- Forge is a bespoke business operating system for trade businesses.
- Do not reposition Forge as a CRM, field-service app, generic job-management platform or AI chatbot.
- Do not rewrite approved positioning without explicit instruction.
- Do not expose We Are Roofing branding, source-company URLs, real customers or private data.
- Use the locked Forge brand tokens and approved imagery.
- Do not introduce generic SaaS patterns, purple AI gradients, bento feature grids, fake testimonials or invented metrics.
- Preserve the recurring demo story: J. Smith / 17 Hawthorn Close / Job 02841 / £8,200 quote / £4,840 invoice.
- The primary CTA is MAP MY BUSINESS.
- Motion must explain business cause → effect.
- Mobile is a separately designed experience, not a stacked desktop page.

## Core rule
IF WE CAN SHOW IT, DON'T EXPLAIN IT.

DON'T DESIGN A PAGE ABOUT FORGE.
MAKE THE PAGE BEHAVE LIKE FORGE.
