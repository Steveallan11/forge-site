# Forge Positioning

## Category
Business Operating System for Trade Businesses.

## Core promise
**YOUR BUSINESS SHOULDN'T NEED YOU TO HOLD IT TOGETHER.**

Supporting outcome:
**LESS OF THE BUSINESS NEEDS TO LIVE IN YOUR HEAD.**

## What Forge is
Forge builds an operating system around the way a trade company actually works.

It connects operational context across enquiries, customers, properties, surveys, photos, reports, quotes, jobs, team activity, documents, invoices, payments, communication and AI-assisted actions.

## What Forge is not
Forge is not simply:
- a CRM
- job-management software
- field-service software
- an AI chatbot
- an automation tool
- a dashboard
- off-the-shelf SaaS
- random custom development

## Commercial framing
**NOT OFF-THE-SHELF SOFTWARE.**
**NOT SOFTWARE BUILT FROM ZERO.**
**A PROVEN FOUNDATION. BUILT AROUND YOUR BUSINESS.**

## Core customer insight
Many trade businesses do not primarily have a software problem. They have an owner-dependency problem.

Too much eventually routes through one person:
- remembering
- approving
- finding
- explaining
- chasing
- connecting

Forge reduces how much of the business needs the owner to manually join everything together.
