## Asset rules
- Do not recolour approved product assets.
- Do not put them inside generic laptop/device mockups unless the composition genuinely needs context.
- Do not stretch them.
- Desktop assets should use art-directed responsive crops.
- Mobile assets are standalone mobile compositions and should not be substituted with scaled desktop screens.
- SVG is intentional: these assets remain crisp at any display size and avoid the low-resolution problem from earlier contact-sheet PNGs.

## Cinematic photography
Cinematic photography remains a separate layer from product proof. Use documentary-style UK trade photography in the physical-world sections only. Keep it realistic, restrained and consistent with the Forge palette. Do not treat generated photography as product evidence.
