Privacy

Privacy information.

Who is responsible? Forge is currently operated by Steve Allan. For questions about your information, contact steveallan2008@gmail.com.

What we collect. If you choose to submit the Forge Blueprint, we receive the answers you selected, your name, company, email address and any phone number you choose to provide. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Why we use it. We use these details to review your business requirements, respond to your enquiry and discuss whether a bespoke Forge implementation may be suitable. We do not use the Blueprint to start an automated marketing sequence.

Legal basis. We process an enquiry to take steps at your request before a possible service agreement and, where appropriate, on the basis of our legitimate interests in responding to genuine business enquiries.

How submissions are delivered. The website is hosted by Vercel. Blueprint submissions are sent through FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, so that the details can be emailed to Steve. FormSubmit's published documentation states that submissions are retained by its service for 30 days. The email delivered to the mailbox may remain there until it is deleted in accordance with mailbox settings and business record needs. These services process information as part of delivering the enquiry.

Who we share it with. We do not sell your details. Information is shared with the service providers described above only as needed to host the site and deliver the enquiry, or where required by law.

How long we keep it. We keep enquiry emails only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain appropriate business records or deal with a follow-up request. We review information that is no longer needed and delete it where appropriate.

Your choices and rights. You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or raise a concern, by emailing steveallan2008@gmail.com. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you believe your information has been handled improperly.

This is an initial privacy notice for the current email-based enquiry setup. It should be reviewed if the data collection, service providers or business arrangements change.
