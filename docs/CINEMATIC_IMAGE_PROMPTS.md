# Forge Cinematic v2 Image Prompts

The production images in `public/cinematic-v2/` were generated individually with the built-in image-generation tool, then converted to WebP at quality 84.

## Shared direction

Use case: `photorealistic-natural`. Premium British trade-business documentary photography; real UK roofing, building, electrical and maintenance environments; 35mm/50mm feel; cool overcast daylight and warm tungsten evenings; natural texture and slight grit. Keep Forge Black, Charcoal and Bone dominant, with Forge Heat used only as a restrained operational accent. Avoid glossy stock photography, US construction cues, robots, holograms, purple gradients, glass cards, watermarks, orange/teal grading and baked marketing headlines.
