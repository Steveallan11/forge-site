# Forge Brand System

## Positioning
Forge is a business operating system for trade businesses, built around the way each company actually works.

## Primary identity
Primary wordmark: **FORGE.**
The full stop is Forge Heat orange and is a core brand device.

Descriptor where required: **BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEMS**

## Palette
- Forge Black — `#0A0C0B`
- Forge Charcoal — `#151817`
- Forge Steel — `#252927`
- Forge Bone — `#F1EEE7`
- Forge Heat — `#FF5A2F`
- Success — `#2DBE78`
- Warning — `#D8A640`
- Danger — `#D95050`

Orange is the Forge brand colour. Green, amber and red are operational state colours only.

## Personality
Industrial. Precise. Premium. British. Operational. Human. Intelligent.

Avoid:
- purple AI gradients
- glowing orbs
- generic construction logos
- anvils, hammers, roofs, gears or AI sparkles in the logo
- excessive glassmorphism
- overly soft rounded SaaS cards

## Typography
### Display
Heavy condensed or engineered sans. Strong, editorial, industrial.

### UI / body
Neutral highly legible sans.

### Operational
Mono or technical condensed type for timestamps, job IDs, money and statuses.

Examples:
`09:14`
`JOB 02841`
`£8,200`
`AWAITING REVIEW`

## UI
- Major panel radius: 12px
- Control radius: 6–8px
- Pills only for genuine statuses
- Accent colour used for action, attention, selection, connection and next-step state
- Dark operating environment with warm Bone contrast
